NoteComplete

A reader and editor for Noteful notes, running entirely in your browser on a Rust core compiled to WebAssembly. Open a .noteful file, PDF or image from your phone or computer: files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Source and docs: noteful-re. Native .noteful export is still experimental.

Open full screen

On a phone, full screen works best. From there you can also add it to your home screen and use it offline.
