A freely shared collection of study notes, problem sets, and exam preparation materials from my first year of Computer Science at ESEI, Universidade de Vigo.

**Context:**

Having completed my first year of Computer Engineering (GREI), I compiled and uploaded all my personal course notes and solved exercises to the Internet Archive under a CC BY 4.0 license.

Includes over 120 documents covering Computer Architecture (ACI), Linear Algebra (AL), Mathematical Foundations (FMI), Programming (PRO I), and Digital Systems (SD) for anyone looking to study or prepare.
